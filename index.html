Hi — I'm Swastik. I work on measurement: whether the numbers we report about a system mean what we say they mean.

Most of what I do is one question asked in different places. When something scores well, is the score measuring the capability it names, or an artifact of how the test was built? That question turns out to be portable — it works on security benchmarks about as well as it works on defense-financing indicators.

I'm at Las Positas on a Berkeley transfer pathway and enrolled at IIT Madras for data science. Lately I've been splitting time between benchmark validity, allied defense financing with MIT's Deep Tech & Geopolitics Lab, and exoplanet detection at NASA Glenn.

I care about reproducibility more than is probably normal, read a lot of semiconductor geopolitics, and think negative results are underrated.
